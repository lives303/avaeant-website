Good questions, clear answers

Before you add something new to your routine.

Product labels are the best source for serving directions, ingredient details, and warnings.

How should I use Avaeant products?

Follow the directions and serving size printed on the specific product label. Do not exceed the suggested use.

Can I combine these products?

Individual needs vary. Review all labels for overlapping ingredients and speak with a qualified healthcare professional before combining supplements.

Who should speak with a healthcare professional first?

Consult a healthcare professional before use if you are pregnant or nursing, under 18, taking medication, have a medical condition, or are planning a medical procedure.

Where can I find complete ingredient information?

Refer to the Supplement Facts and ingredient panel on the product you receive. Packaging is the controlling source for current formula and serving information.
